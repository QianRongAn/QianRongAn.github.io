Experience's cover and first role now show nanoinjection / immune-cell delivery; the later specimens show cell communication and cohort / alteration analysis.
The banyan ending uses the generated landscape as an image-based depth projection, with subtle foliage movement and scroll-driven camera drift. This preserves the detailed generated trunk, aerial roots, daylight and lawn; it is not a fully modeled walk-around tree or an AI-generated video. The existing fly-through gradually reaches daylight and pauses at the literature beats while the six original paper meshes retain their textures and wind. The approved image is unchanged; no tint or pale wash is applied.

The tree image and its shader are prepared before the opening particle film. The particle film itself remains the original 3840 x 2160 asset. About's independent crystal butterfly is unchanged.
