The site remains a vanilla JavaScript / Three.js site. These are adapted interaction patterns, not imported React components or copies of the reference websites.

The three original roles, dates and nineteen responsibilities are retained. One shared WebGL canvas follows the visible specimen. The original SVGs remain as fallback artwork.

Projects have eight distinct procedural concept scenes: contact graph / peptide, evidence sheets, interacting PDAC cells, DNA fragments, protein pocket / ligand, epithelial barrier, genomic helices and immune binding. Wheel progress moves a virtual camera through three phases with stationary reading intervals. Arrow keys, Page Up / Down and Home / End offer the same exploration. At a progress boundary, wheel input returns to normal page scrolling. Published figures, summaries and metrics are retained.

The banyan ending uses the generated landscape as an image-based depth projection, with subtle foliage movement and scroll-driven camera drift. This preserves the detailed generated trunk, aerial roots, daylight and lawn; it is not a fully modeled walk-around tree or an AI-generated video. The existing fly-through arrives and pauses at the literature beats while the six original paper meshes retain their textures and wind.

The tree image and its shader are prepared before the opening particle film. The particle film itself remains the original 3840 x 2160 asset. About's independent crystal butterfly is unchanged.
