Changes: extracted the caudal region, removed the proximal museum support wire,
applied a rigid axis conversion and uniform scaling, converted to GLB, and
reduced 355,187 extracted triangles to 39,985 triangles using meshoptimizer.
Relative simplification error: 0.0004883 (about 0.049% of model extent).
The scanned fork, proportions and asymmetry are retained; no invented fin rays
or non-uniform deformation was added. The display material is iridescent metal.
This is a prepared museum skeleton surface scan, not a CT segmentation.

The website combines this fish caudal region with a separate human spine as
an artistic assemblage. It does not depict an organism occurring in nature.
